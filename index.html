How I work

A simple process, so you always know what’s next

  1. 1

    Discover

    We talk about your goals, your audience and what you already have: a logo, content, examples you like.

  2. 2

    Plan

    I send a short outline or concept direction. We agree on what will be made before any design starts.

  3. 3

    Design & build

    I share drafts as I go, you give feedback, and I refine until it is right.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    You get the final files or the finished site, plus a handover so you can use it with confidence.
