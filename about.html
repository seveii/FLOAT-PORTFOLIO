Hello, I'm

Rafael Joseph Nieves

Computer Engineering student, web designer & graphic artist based in Marilao, Bulacan.

I'm a 21-year-old 3rd Year BS Computer Engineering student at Dr. Yanga's Colleges Inc. Somewhere between wiring circuits for class and staying up designing layouts, I found out the thing I actually love is building things people get to look at and use — mostly websites, sometimes brand identities, always with too much attention to detail.

Outside of school I'm usually cafe hopping with a camera, shooting everything in black & white, planning the next trip to somewhere new, or grinding a few rounds online when I finally get free time. I've served as Multimedia Director for the Association of Computer Enthusiast Student, and I'm currently P.R.O. of ICpEP — so leading creative work for a room full of people is pretty normal for me at this point.
