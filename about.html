About

A designer who also understands how things are built

Hi, I’m Rafael. I’m a third-year Computer Engineering student at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges and a consistent Dean’s Lister.

I combine technical skills with a designer’s eye. I build websites, and I design the logos, posters and content that make an organization look professional and consistent.

For the past few years I have led multimedia and communications for student organizations. I was Multimedia Director of the Association of Computer Enthusiast Students, and I am now the Public Relations Officer of ICpEP. That means real deadlines, real audiences and teams relying on the work.

I also use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT and Lovable for research, coding and image and video ideas. They speed up the early steps, but the final design decisions are mine.

Away from the screen you’ll find me café hopping, travelling to places I love, and playing the occasional online game.
